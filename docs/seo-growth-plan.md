## Content backlog

Start with two substantive articles per month as a proposed cadence, not an activated schedule. Draft from real expertise, use clear definitions and practical steps, and link to the relevant service and contact page.

1. Which intake KPIs should a law firm track? Define contact, booking, show, and hire rates with consistent denominators.
2. What does a practice growth audit examine? Explain inputs, bottlenecks, deliverables, and next steps.
3. When should a practice hire fractional sales leadership? Explain fit, responsibilities, and engagement limits.
4. How do you find lost opportunities between marketing and intake? Provide an operational diagnostic.

No invented outcomes, testimonials, clients, credentials, or benchmarks. Real case studies require approved source evidence and permission to publish identifying details. Dashboard artwork is not case-study evidence.

## Credibility and citations

Verify official business contact information and public professional profiles before linking them in structured data. Evaluate Google Business Profile eligibility based on actual customer contact and operating model. Prioritize relevant legitimate professional associations, partner references, and business profiles. Do not buy ranking links, mass-submit directories, or invent addresses.

## Proposed operating process

- Weekly: review indexing, sitemap status, broken links, crawl restrictions, and new search queries.
- Monthly: review qualified inquiries, landing pages, impressions, clicks, CTR, query positions, and content opportunities. Refresh existing useful pages before adding redundant pages.
- Before publishing: factual review, source check, metadata and canonical validation, internal-link review, mobile inspection, and deployed-page verification.
- Quarterly: review service priorities, evidence, conversion paths, and measured performance.

No recurring automation, independent agents, paid subscriptions, outreach, or account changes activated. Search Console dashboard access is not connected to an automated reporting tool. Page-view collection is confirmed; conversion-event collection remains unverified.
