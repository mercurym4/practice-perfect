When improvement keeps getting postponed

The same concern may come up in meeting after meeting. A project may start with enthusiasm and then lose attention as other priorities take over. Leadership may feel caught between leaving the problem in place and committing to a much larger change than the business needs.

Delay has a business cost. The team continues to carry the frustration, prospective clients experience the same friction, and owners remain uncertain about progress. At the same time, trying to change everything at once can stretch a practice beyond its available capacity.

A focused engagement may be a better fit

Practice Perfect’s Revenue Growth Sprint is an option for practices seeking a focused engagement around growth priorities. The appropriate work depends on the agreed scope, the business need, and the team’s capacity to participate.

A sprint is not a promise that every constraint will disappear. It is a way to discuss a defined engagement when leadership wants support moving a priority forward. If the main problem is still unclear, an audit may be a more useful starting point.
