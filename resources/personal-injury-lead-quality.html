More Personal Injury Inquiries Are Not Always the Same as Better Opportunities

For a personal injury practice, the value of a new inquiry depends on whether it relates to the firm’s services and can progress to a meaningful conversation. A large volume of inquiries can create work without creating the growth leadership expected.

When volume becomes a distraction

A team may spend much of its time responding to people whose needs fall outside the firm’s practice. Leadership may feel pressure to keep increasing demand while remaining uncertain about whether the existing opportunities fit the business.

A disappointing outcome does not automatically establish a lead-quality problem. The firm’s capacity and the prospective client experience also matter. Those concerns deserve attention before treating volume alone as evidence of success.
