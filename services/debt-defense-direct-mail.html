Questions about Debt Defense Direct Mail

Who is this service for?

This service is for debt defense firms considering direct mail as part of their acquisition efforts. The right fit depends on the firm’s services, business goals, and capacity to handle prospective client conversations.

How much does Debt Defense Direct Mail cost?

This is a custom acquisition program. Pricing varies with market, data, volume, and campaign requirements. Request a scoped quote rather than assuming a standard package or price per inquiry.

What support should we discuss?

The published offering includes campaign planning, creative, production, postage, tracking, and intake alignment. Confirm the proposed scope and costs before selecting an engagement.

Will a campaign guarantee responses or new clients?

No response rate or client volume is promised. Marketing interest is not the same as a client engagement, and results also depend on service fit and inquiry handling.

Who approves the firm’s marketing communications?

The firm retains responsibility for its professional obligations and approval of marketing communications. Campaign requirements should be addressed with the firm before publication.
