Common Questions

Before you book.

What is fractional sales leadership?

It is an ongoing engagement that provides sales and intake leadership without a full-time commitment. Practice Perfect focuses on structure, coaching, scripts, standards, KPIs, and performance accountability.

How much does it cost?

Fractional Sales Leadership starts at $3,500 per month. Final pricing depends on the team, responsibilities, and support required. Compare the current engagement options and discuss scope before making a commitment.

Is this only for law firms?

No. Practice Perfect supports healthcare practices, law firms, and high-value service businesses. The engagement is built around the way your team handles inquiries, sales conversations, follow-up, and reporting.

How is this different from a Revenue Growth Sprint?

A Revenue Growth Sprint is a focused implementation engagement with a 90-day execution plan. Fractional Sales Leadership provides ongoing team leadership, coaching, standards, and performance management. The right option depends on whether your main need is implementation or sustained leadership.

How many hours or meetings are included?

The website does not define a fixed time allocation. Leadership responsibilities, availability, meetings, and monthly scope are agreed for the engagement. The starting price should not be read as a promise of a particular number of hours.

Do we need an audit first?

Not every engagement needs to begin with an audit. If the constraint or available reporting is unclear, a Practice Growth Audit can help prioritize the work. The first conversation establishes the appropriate entry point.
