Common Questions

Before you book.

What is law firm intake consulting?

It is operational support for the process between a prospective client inquiry and the firm’s next decision. Practice Perfect focuses on response, scripts, qualification, consultation booking, follow-up, CRM workflows, and performance visibility.

Is intake consulting the same as buying leads?

No. Lead generation creates opportunities. Intake consulting focuses on how your firm handles those opportunities. Better handling and reporting help you understand whether the constraint is demand, conversion, or team execution.

Can you work with our existing CRM?

The first conversation should cover your CRM, pipeline stages, and available reporting. CRM structure and discipline are part of our growth work. Any software-specific implementation is agreed in scope rather than assumed.

How is intake consulting priced?

The appropriate engagement depends on the work required. A Practice Growth Audit starts at $1,500, a Revenue Growth Sprint starts at $5,000, and Fractional Sales Leadership starts at $3,500 per month. See our current engagement options; these are starting prices, not a fixed intake package.

Does Practice Perfect provide legal advice or decide which cases to accept?

No. This work concerns business processes and team performance. Your firm sets legal criteria, approves intake questions and messaging, and makes case-acceptance decisions.

Where should we start if the problem is unclear?

Start with a strategy conversation. A Practice Growth Audit may be the right entry point when you need to establish the constraint before choosing implementation or ongoing support.
