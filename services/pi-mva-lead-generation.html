Questions about PI / MVA Lead Generation

Who is PI / MVA Lead Generation for?

The service is for personal injury and motor vehicle accident practices seeking specialized lead-generation support. A conversation can establish whether the offering fits your market and growth priorities.

How are programs priced?

This is a custom acquisition program. Market, volume, qualification, and exclusivity can materially affect scope and pricing. Discuss the proposed terms before making a commitment.

What is the Max-Settlement connection?

Practice Perfect’s published offering describes MVA lead-generation programs through its Max-Settlement partnership. Discuss the specific program, responsibilities, and requirements as part of your engagement conversation.

Are inquiries guaranteed cases or settlements?

No. An inquiry is not a retained matter. No case volume, settlement outcome, or revenue is guaranteed.

Who decides whether an inquiry becomes a client matter?

The firm retains responsibility for legal evaluation, case acceptance, professional obligations, and its client relationships. Lead-generation interest does not replace those decisions.
