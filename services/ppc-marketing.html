Questions about PPC Marketing

What does PPC Marketing focus on?

Practice Perfect offers paid-search management built around acquisition efficiency. The engagement should fit the services your practice offers and the client opportunities it wants to attract.

How much does PPC Marketing cost?

PPC Marketing starts at $1,500+ per month. Confirm the management scope and advertising budget before selecting an engagement.

Does the starting price cover all advertising costs?

Do not assume that it does. The published price is a starting point for PPC Marketing. Discuss management fees, media spend, and any other proposed costs before agreeing to the scope.

Can PPC guarantee a number of leads or a return?

No. Paid marketing should not be described as a guarantee of a particular lead volume, acquisition cost, or return. Results depend on the market, offer, website experience, and inquiry handling.

What if inquiries are arriving but not becoming clients?

Advertising may not be the only concern. Your practice may benefit from broader growth or intake support. Discuss where the business needs help before deciding to increase spending.
