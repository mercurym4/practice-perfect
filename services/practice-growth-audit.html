What We Review

Connect the full path
from inquiry to revenue.

The audit looks at how the stages work together. More leads may help, but the first constraint could be response, follow-up, conversion, or reporting.

01

Marketing & lead quality

Review where inquiries originate, how they fit your services, and whether source reporting helps explain acquisition performance.

02

Intake & follow-up

Examine response, call handling, qualification, scheduling, and follow-up to understand where inquiries stop progressing.

03

Sales process

Review handoffs, consultation steps, conversion tracking, and team ownership from first conversation to a client decision.

04

CRM & workflow

Examine pipeline stages, assigned next steps, and follow-up processes to see whether the system supports consistent execution.

05

Revenue visibility

Check how lead sources, conversion, and revenue are connected, including gaps that make performance difficult to interpret.

Common Questions

Before you book.

What is a Practice Growth Audit?

It is a focused diagnostic of the path from marketing and lead generation through intake, sales, CRM, and revenue reporting. The engagement includes bottleneck analysis, a funnel review, and a prioritized action plan.

How much does the audit cost?

The Practice Growth Audit starts at $1,500. The final price depends on the scope of the review. We discuss your needs and confirm the engagement before work begins. You can compare the current options on our Pricing page.

Is this only for healthcare practices?

No. Practice Perfect supports healthcare practices, law firms, and high-value service businesses. The review focuses on how your business attracts inquiries, handles opportunities, converts demand, and measures results.

Does the audit include implementation?

The audit is the diagnostic engagement. Implementation is scoped separately, such as a Revenue Growth Sprint, while ongoing team leadership can be addressed through Fractional Sales Leadership.

What if our reporting is incomplete?

Tell us what information is available in the first conversation. Gaps in source, conversion, or revenue reporting help define the review. Recommendations should distinguish what the data supports from what still needs measurement.

Does the audit guarantee revenue growth?

No. The audit identifies constraints and recommends actions. Outcomes depend on factors including your market, lead quality, available data, implementation, and team execution.
