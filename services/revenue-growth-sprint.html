Questions about the Revenue Growth Sprint

How is a sprint different from the Practice Growth Audit?

The audit is a diagnostic engagement with a prioritized action plan. The sprint is an implementation engagement focused on acquisition and conversion constraints.

Does the sprint include a 90-day execution plan?

Yes. The existing engagement includes a 90-day execution plan, process and funnel improvements, and a KPI and reporting structure. Specific priorities are discussed as part of your scope.

Is this the same as ongoing fractional leadership?

No. The sprint is a focused implementation engagement. Fractional Sales Leadership is ongoing support for teams that need structure, coaching, KPIs, and accountability.
