Questions about SEO Marketing

Who is SEO Marketing for?

Practice Perfect serves healthcare practices, law firms, and high-value service businesses seeking search visibility that supports relevant client interest.

How much does SEO Marketing cost?

SEO Marketing starts at $2,000+ per month. The appropriate scope depends on your business needs. Review the engagement before committing.

Can you guarantee a Google ranking or AI recommendation?

No. An SEO engagement does not guarantee a particular search position, inclusion in an AI-generated answer, or a fixed number of clients.

Should we choose SEO or PPC?

SEO focuses on search visibility and content, while PPC involves paid-search management. Your goals, website, market, and budget affect the choice. The services should not be treated as interchangeable.

Will more website traffic solve a conversion problem?

Not necessarily. The relevance of visitors, the website experience, and how your team handles inquiries also affect client conversations. Broader growth support may fit when visibility is only one part of the concern.
