Questions about Website Audit + Overhaul

What does Website Audit + Overhaul focus on?

The engagement focuses on improving messaging, trust, calls to action, lead capture, and conversion flow on an existing website.

How much does the engagement cost?

Website Audit + Overhaul starts at $2,500+. The appropriate scope depends on your current website and the improvements your practice needs.

Is this the same as the Practice Growth Audit?

No. Website Audit + Overhaul focuses on the website experience and improvements. The Practice Growth Audit is a separate diagnostic engagement covering broader growth concerns, including marketing, intake, sales, CRM, and revenue visibility.

Does an overhaul guarantee more clients?

No. Website improvements can support a clearer visitor experience, but outcomes also depend on demand, traffic, your services, and how inquiries are handled.
