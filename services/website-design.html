When a new website may be the right investment

A new website may be appropriate when your current site no longer reflects the business you want clients to see. You may have changed your services, outgrown an older presentation, or found that the site makes it difficult to explain what your practice offers.

The question is whether the site supports the client’s decision. A new design should serve a business need, rather than change the appearance alone.

Questions about Website Design

Who is Website Design for?

Practice Perfect serves healthcare practices, law firms, and high-value service businesses. The engagement focuses on a website that helps prospective clients understand the business and take a clear next step.

How much does Website Design cost?

Website Design starts at $4,500+. Discuss your needs and project scope before selecting an engagement.

Should we rebuild or improve the current site?

A new build may fit when your current site cannot support the presentation your business needs. Website Audit + Overhaul may fit when an existing site can be improved. The choice depends on the actual condition of the site and your business goals.

Will a new website guarantee more leads?

No. Website design supports how your business presents itself and handles visitor interest. Results also depend on demand, traffic, your offer, and how your team responds to inquiries.
